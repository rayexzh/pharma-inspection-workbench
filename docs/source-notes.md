# Source notes

Reviewed against the live official pages on **4 October 2026**. This is a limited reading record, not a complete GMP/GDP requirements set. MHRA stands for Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency; GMP and GDP refer to good manufacturing and good distribution practice.

## S-RESPONSE — Post-inspection responses

**MHRA, “Responding to a GMP/GDP post inspection letter.”** Published 12 February 2019; last updated 8 January 2021.

[Official GOV.UK page](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/guidance-on-responding-to-a-gmpgdp-post-inspection-letter)

Relevant sections: 1.2, initial site response and second site response; section 2, missed commitments. The page covers specific actions and dates, wider scope, interim measures, editable responses and preservation of earlier replies. It says: “not provide evidence of what you have done, unless specifically asked to”.

**Design implication:** maintain an internal evidence index, without automatically attaching completed-action evidence. Markdown exports are internal reports, not the editable Word reply requested in the guidance. The app's local history does not implement a controlled official correspondence record.

## S-AI — AI-assisted inspection responses

**MHRA Inspectorate, “Use of AI for GXP inspection responses: setting standards without stifling innovation.”** Published 29 June 2026.

[Official MHRA Inspectorate article](https://mhrainspectorate.blog.gov.uk/2026/06/29/use-of-ai-for-gxp-inspection-responses-setting-standards-without-stifling-innovation/)

Relevant sections: “What hasn't changed”, “Clarifications” and “Voluntary disclosure”. The article emphasises accurate, verifiable content, experienced technical review and accountable approval, including when drafting uses AI. AI disclosure is voluntary in that article.

**Design implication:** keep sources beside drafts and demonstrate a review step. A typed demo reviewer does not satisfy real approval arrangements. The software checks completeness, not factual accuracy. Project AI disclosure is an honest portfolio practice, not a claim of mandatory regulator disclosure.

## S-INVESTIGATION — Investigations and corrective action

**MHRA Inspectorate, “A fresh look at an old topic: Investigations in the GMDP environment.”** Published 14 September 2020.

[Official MHRA Inspectorate article](https://mhrainspectorate.blog.gov.uk/2020/09/14/a-fresh-look-at-an-old-topic-investigations-in-the-gmdp-environment/)

Relevant sections: “With the investigation”, “Effective investigations”, “Targeted CAPAs” and “A well-managed system”. It discusses evidence-led investigation, recurring problems, alternatives to assumed human error, actions addressing root causes and follow-up.

**Design implication:** separate root cause, impact, actions and effectiveness records. The app's closure guard is a learning rule. A completed result field is not a scientific assessment of effectiveness or a mandated universal closure test.
